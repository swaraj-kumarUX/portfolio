About Me

At redBus I lead end-to-end UX for operator and customer-facing features and scale the design system across platforms. Along the way I've built enterprise design systems, shipped B2B products, and obsessed over the smallest interaction details — pairing a design-thinking mindset with an MBA in marketing & analytics.
